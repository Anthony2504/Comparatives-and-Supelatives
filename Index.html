Grammar reference

Comparative (comparing 2 things)

Subject + verb + adjective-er + than + comparison

A cheetah is faster than a horse.

Superlative (the most / the -est)

Subject + verb + the + adjective-est

The cheetah is the fastest animal.

Spelling rules

  • Most short adjectives: add -er or -est. Example: fast → faster → the fastest.
  • Consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter. Example: big → bigger → the biggest.
  • Ends in -y: change y to i. Example: heavy → heavier → the heaviest.
Remember: Use than with comparatives (faster than). Use the with superlatives (the fastest).

Word list
